5. READING — what Phase A established (and how it re-prioritizes Phases B–D)
================================================================================
The gap is UPSTREAM of "an additive marginal can't represent the product" (the plan's
lead hypothesis). The biggest, cleanest, most run-consistent conversion disagreement is
ECONOMIC DISCIPLINE: the engine dissipates money into diffuse pack/reroll RNG (its top
spend actions) while winners BANK (interest discipline) and spend selectively on directed
pieces. This is exactly the `human-economy-policy` spec made measurable ("satisfice
survival, invest surplus, econ gated by remaining need, interest discipline").

RE-PRIORITIZED PLAN (supersedes the B/C/D order in CONVERSION_PLAN.md §3):
  B' (NEW #1) — PACK/REROLL SPEND-APPETITE vs BANKING. Why is BUY_PACK the engine's top
     buy (23%) and why does it never skip (95%)? FIRST check whether this is a REGRESSION
     from this session's econ/pack-first work (git bisect the pack-appetite over ADVICE
     commits) — a recent over-correction is a cheap fix. Then, if structural: the pack EV
     vs the banked-interest value + the "save for a directed keystone" option. Measure on
     eng_buy_human_save / eng_reroll_human_save falling toward the human's ~2%.
  C' — the forward marginal on the committed target board (old Phase C): the "which joker"
     gap (buy-agreement 29%). Still the right representational fix for the 36%-multiplier
     punch-list, but SECOND now.
  D' — pack-PICK conversion (old Phase D): once the engine opens fewer/better packs, the
     37.5% pick agreement — shape/money/level vs enhance.
  B(completion model) folds into C' (both are "value the product-completing piece").

CAVEATS (do not over-claim):
  * Window inference: in the 3 NON-rich runs a human pack-buy reads as "SAVE" until opened.
    The pack over-buy is confirmed in the 4 RICH runs, so it is real — but the exact rate
    carries inference noise; trust DIRECTION over the point estimate.
  * This is the CURRENT engine (post-fidelity, post-econ-system, post pack-first-sequencing).
    The pack over-buy may be partly a 2026-08-28 regression — check before building (B').
  * n=7 winning runs. In-sample. Any selected constant gets an OOS confirmation
    (winner's curse is the documented killer — §32c).
  * Autonomous sim winrate remains the SAFETY RAIL, not the target: a conversion change
    ships if it moves these agreement numbers toward the human AND is not significantly
    negative on the paired tail-verdict.
