lead hypothesis). The biggest, cleanest, most run-consistent conversion disagreement is

spend actions) while winners BANK (interest discipline) and spend selectively on directed
pieces. This is exactly the `human-economy-policy` spec made measurable ("satisfice

B' EXECUTED (2026-08-29) — pack/reroll spend-appetite vs banking
================================================================================
HYPOTHESIS (owner): "engine buys packs too much when poor, not enough when rich."
CONFIRMED in money-bucketed data (rich runs, engine vs human pack-buy rate):
  $5-9 poor      engine 17%  human 6%      below cap   engine 16-27%  human 10-11%
  at cap $25-39  engine 20%  human 18%     rich $40+   engine 25-27%  human 17-20%
  => engine pack appetite is money-FLAT; the human's RISES with money (banks poor, spends rich).

ROOT CAUSE FOUND (and reverted): an interest_cap UNIT MISMATCH. The engine's economy model
uses interest_cap in INTEREST-DOLLARS (5/10/20; M.RESERVE = cap*5); the sim honors it, but the
LIVE G.GAME.interest_cap is the DOLLAR threshold (25/50/100 — confirmed in fixtures) and
state_extraction copied it RAW. So the live overlay computed reserve targets 5x too high and
over-charged the interest cost of spending when rich. FIXING it (Mechanics.norm_interest_cap at
the POD boundary) was CORRECT but measured a large agreement REGRESSION on the harness:
  after (interest fix only): eng_reroll_human_save +10.9pp, agree_save -11.4pp.
WHY: the winners bank PAST the mechanical interest cap (option-value for a directed keystone,
not interest optimization), and the OLD reroll/spend appetite was tuned around the bug (its
5x-too-high reserve accidentally reproduced the human's over-banking). Four option-value-banking
variants (bank_target reroll gate; pack damp; bankroll hold-target; reroll discipline) each
recovered ~1.5pp but NONE beat base (after..after4: reroll +10.9 -> +9.5 -> +7.7 -> +6.4).
=> the interest fix + banking thread was REVERTED (git reset to the harness commit). The finding
stands as future work: shipping the correct interest math requires a validated option-value
banking model (bank a war chest, taper in the endgame), tuned on a larger corpus with OOS
confirmation, NOT on n=7.

SHIPPED (the one clean lever, 20c146c, deployed live): PACK-POVERTY DISCIPLINE. Damp a
speculative pack's POOL value below the max-interest bankroll (steeper-than-linear ^1.6, floor
0.3), never when urgent, never the owned-synergy term. Dollar threshold is convention-robust
((cap>=25)?cap:cap*5 = $25/50/100 on BOTH live and sim), so no broad interest change needed.
  agreement (7-win corpus): eng_buy_human_save 264->258, NO channel regressed (agree_save +0.3).
  winrate rail (paired n=48 vs clean base): Δ -0.042 progress (p 0.87 n.s.), wins 3=3, tail n.s.
  => SAFE + advice-positive (the econ-work profile). Modest but correct-direction and no harm.
CAVEAT: agreement gain is small and in-sample (n=7); the change is DIRECTION-motivated (human
buys fewer poor packs, a robust measured fact) not a magnitude fit. Rail is a coarse n=48 safety
screen (MDE ~0.74) — sufficient to rule out a large regression, not a small one.

NEXT (unchanged): C' forward marginal (the 29% which-joker gap); D' pack-PICK conversion (37.5%).

C' SCOPED -> REFUTED (2026-08-29) — joker VALUATION is not the gap
================================================================================
C' hypothesized the engine under-picks multiplier keystones because it values them on
the thin CURRENT board (a forward-marginal fix). Two scoping steps killed it:

1. ANTE DATA refutes the naive forward projection. The human buys multipliers LATE
   (antes 5-6 = 10 of 14; antes 1-2 = 1, with 15 additive) — confirming §5o "winners are
   xmult-POOR early". A forward projection boosts multipliers EARLY (long runway) = the
   LOSING pattern. Refuted before building (the §31d rule).

2. RANKED-VALUATION DIAGNOSTIC (tools/cprime_diag.lua) refutes the valuation gap itself.
   Of 60 decisions where the human bought a joker AND the engine ranked it:
     AGREE (human's joker = engine's rank-1 JOKER): 47 (78%)
     MULTIPLIER human-picks: 21/23 AGREE (91%)   ADDITIVE: 26/37 agree
     UNDER-MEASURED (valued <=0 or below tier): 0
   The engine ranks the human's multiplier #1 (Photograph 25, Hanging Chad 20, Cavendish
   19, Blueprint 2648, ...) — it does NOT under-value multipliers. The earlier "engine
   picks additive 100% over multiplier" was an ARTIFACT of comparing the engine's overall
   BEST action (often a pack) instead of its joker ranking.

=> C' is REFUTED. Relative joker pricing is directionally correct (confirms §5s / the
   divergence mine, now on the AGREEMENT instrument). The "which-joker" gap (29% buy-agree
   from Phase A) is NOT valuation — it is the engine preferring a PACK over the human's
   directed joker buy 39% of the time (Phase A §3), i.e. the SAME spend-discipline gap as
   B'. The conversion frontier collapses toward ECONOMY/SPEND DISCIPLINE (B', partially
   shipped as pack-poverty) and the pack-PICK channel (D'), NOT joker valuation (C').

CONVERSION INVESTIGATION — OVERALL CONCLUSION (2026-08-29)
================================================================================
Measured on the AGREEMENT instrument (the non-ceilinged advice-quality ruler), the
conversion gap is NOT what CONVERSION_PLAN.md hypothesized:
  * NOT product-scoring / target-modeling (§32 target model already OOS-failed; not re-opened).
  * NOT joker VALUATION (C' REFUTED — engine ranks the human's multiplier #1 91% of the time).
  * NOT pack-PICK card choice (D' is within-category + tied to the hard commitment problem).
The gap is ECONOMY / SPEND DISCIPLINE: the engine dissipates money on packs/rerolls where the
winners BANK (option-value, past the mechanical interest cap) and buy DIRECTED pieces. This is
consistent across Phase A (#1 gap = pack over-buy), B' (the interest-cap unit bug + the
option-value banking finding) and C'/D' (which collapse back into it).
SHIPPED: pack-poverty discipline (bank toward the cap before gambling on a pack) — safe +
advice-positive, the clean slice of the economy lever.
OPEN (future, needs a BIGGER corpus + OOS discipline — winner's-curse risk on n=7): the full
OPTION-VALUE BANKING model (bank a war chest past the mechanical cap, taper in the endgame),
paired with the (correct) interest_cap unit fix so the two co-calibrate instead of fighting.
The durable asset is tools/agreement_run.sh — every future economy change gets a clean
before/after against the winners.
